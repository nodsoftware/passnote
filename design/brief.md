# Brief: Conclave logo

## Product

Conclave is an end-to-end encrypted, server-less, local-first sync library for
Dart and Flutter. A small, closed group of devices shares one live dataset; no
plaintext leaves a device. Source: `README.md`, `docs/index.md`,
`docs/security.md`.

## Audience

Dart and Flutter developers choosing a sync layer, and the security reviewers
who vet it.

## Personality

Trustworthy, quiet, precise. Not playful, not "hacker".

## Idea

The name comes from Latin *cum clave*, "with a key": a room locked so that only
the members inside can talk. The mark is a C (the closed group) wrapped around
a keyhole (the shared key).

## Requirements

- Reads at 16 px (pub.dev, GitHub avatar, favicon).
- Works in one colour and reversed on dark.
- No gradients, no purple, no padlock clip art.
